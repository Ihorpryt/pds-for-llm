Tabs

Horizontal only — 2 sizes × selected on/off × 5 states, with optional icon, badge, chevron and close slots, scroll buttons, and a Glass variant. All values resolve through tokens.css.

Sizes

Selected × State

Rendered with the forced-state helpers .is-selected, .is-hover, .is-active, .is-focus, .is-disabled.

SizeSelectedDefaultHoverActiveFocusDisabled

Slots

Leading icon, a .psds-badge count, a dropdown chevron and a close affix — each optional.

Glass

.psds-tabs--glass — translucent pills for a coloured background such as the secondary navigation bar. States, top to bottom: live, then forced hover, focus and disabled.

Interactive

A working tab list with panels. Arrow keys, Home and End move between tabs; the scroll buttons appear because the bar is narrower than its tabs.
